Paper I: Jurisprudence

The whole paper for LL.B. 1st Year, First Semester, Lucknow University, in four units and fifteen short, interactive lessons. Start with what jurisprudence is, work through the great schools of legal theory and the basic concepts of law, and finish with justice and the law's moral limits. Every unit closes with an exam kit of likely questions, answer skeletons and flashcards.

Unit III: the building blocks of law

Every branch of law, from contract to crime, is built from a handful of basic ideas. This unit takes three of them apart in three interactive lessons: rights and duties, ownership and possession, and legal personality. The aim is that you can define each precisely, classify its kinds, and back every point with a jurist and a case.

The lessons

Read them in order. The first gives you the vocabulary (right, duty, claim, power); the second applies it to things; the third asks who can hold rights at all. Each ends with a quick self-check.

Unit IV: justice, and the law's moral limits

The first three units asked what law is and where it comes from. This one asks whether it is any good. In three interactive lessons you'll cover the great theories of justice from Aristotle to Rawls and Nozick, the clash between modernism and postmodernism in legal thought, and the old, still-burning question of law and morality, from the Wolfenden Report to Navtej Singh Johar.

The lessons

Read them in order. The first asks what a just distribution looks like; the second asks whether law can be neutral and universal at all; the third asks how far law may enforce morality, and whose. Each ends with a quick self-check.
